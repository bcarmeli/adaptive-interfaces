## Working practice

Read the relevant BNL context before making conceptual decisions. Keep operational experiment details and authored drafts here. Promote durable cross-project conclusions into BNL deliberately as self-contained summaries with non-link provenance; record the returned BNL commit and destinations locally. No automatic synchronization or reverse dependencies.

The former wiki is frozen and excluded from current context. The [cutover receipt](manifests/bnl-cutover-receipt.md) records the verified migration and archive hashes. Authored drafts retain their original text and may contain older claims; consult the BNL context for current qualifications.
