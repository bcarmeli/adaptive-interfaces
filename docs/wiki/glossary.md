# Glossary

## Affordance

An action or collaboration possibility exposed to a particular sender by a receiver. Affordances are user-relative: the same receiver and interface may afford different capabilities to different senders.

## Communicative Interface

The observable surface through which an agent exposes signals, claims, examples, explanations, symbols, APIs, or other public handles. The interface is not the latent space itself.

## Latent Space

The receiver's private internal representations, capabilities, heuristics, reasoning processes, or code-level behavior.

## Latent Capability

Something the receiver can do internally or operationally, whether or not it is exposed to the sender.

## Task-Relevant Affordance

A perceived and usable capability of the receiver relative to the sender's current task.

## Mutual Interpretability

The degree to which sender and receiver can use the communicative interface to understand and coordinate with each other.

## Faithful Self-Presentation

Interface behavior that exposes capabilities in a way that remains useful during downstream task execution. This is an instrumental property, not a moral category.

## Unfaithful Self-Presentation

Interface behavior that increases selection probability while failing to expose capabilities that remain usable during downstream task execution. This is an instrumental property, not a moral category.

## Discrete Reference

A public handle with a distinguishable identity that lets agents identify and revisit a task-relevant target. Words, names, and API identifiers are typical examples. Examples and demonstrations may support reference but are not inherently discrete. A continuous carrier can encode discrete references.

## Addressability

The ability to identify or access a task-relevant target through an interface. Addressability alone does not imply a discrete signal or a discrete set of targets; continuous coordinates and pointing can also support it.

## Referential Identity

The criterion by which interlocutors treat two mentions as concerning the same target, even as its properties change. Exact robust recovery of distinct identities imposes separation requirements under the assumptions in [[interface-affordance-and-reference]].
